Support

The Body Guard app · Updated November 2, 2026

Get in touch

In the app: open The Body Guard, go to Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Contact Us. Pick a reason, write your message and send. If something broke, pick Report a bug: it adds the app version, your iPhone model and a short activity log to help fix it. The log never includes your photos, and it never names a body area.

Messages go straight to the developer, Brad Bernasek. You’ll get an answer by email.

Common questions

Where are my photos?

Scan photos are saved to your Photos library as Hidden photos. In Photos, open Albums › Hidden and unlock with Face ID. The app keeps its own copy too, so it can compare each new photo with the last one of the same spot.

I got a new phone, or lost mine. How do I get my history back?

The app’s data, including your photo copies, labels, flags and history, is part of a normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or your computer). Restore the new phone from that backup and it comes back with the app. Your sign-in is kept in the iPhone’s secure Keychain on that one phone and does not move to a new one, so open the app and create your account again with the same email.

The app also keeps one encrypted file of your labels, flags and history. Settings › Account › Save a copy sends it to a place you pick (Files, a Mac, anywhere), and Settings › Account › Restore from a copy brings it back. It opens with the password you had when you saved the copy, or with your Recovery Key. Keep your Recovery Key somewhere safe: without it or your password, nobody can open that file, the developer included. Your photos stay in Photos and in the app’s own copy; a restored label whose photo is only in your Hidden album is listed as such, because the app can’t read your Photos library.

The full-body scan reminder never shows up

If you turned on Only ask when I’m there, the reminder only appears when you are at the place you picked and inside the time window you set, so you don’t get asked at work. Check three things: the scan days, time and place in Settings › General › Full-body scan schedule; Location set to Always for The Body Guard in iPhone Settings; and notifications turned on.

I skipped a scan. Will it ask again?

Yes. Skip asks when to remind you next, with a sensible time already picked, so scans never just stop.

How do I delete everything?

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete account and all data. That erases your account, photos, scans, notes and settings on this iPhone, the backup the app keeps on this iPhone, and your backup in the folder you chose if you set one up. Then delete the photos in Photos › Albums › Hidden, because the app can add photos there but not remove them. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does the developer see my photos?

No. Photos and health details stay on your iPhone. The only thing that reaches the developer is a Contact Us message you choose to send. See the privacy policy.

The Body Guard does not diagnose anything. It helps you track your skin over time. Always see a doctor or dermatologist about any spot that changes, and before making any medical decision. In an emergency, call 911.
