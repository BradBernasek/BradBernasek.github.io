About Brad

I'm Brad Bernasek. Currently Director of Engineering at Adios Golf Club in Coconut Creek, Florida. Career has been one long arc through operations, logistics, and facilities — across medical equipment, full-service logistics, higher education, and hospitality.

The work has changed sectors but the core has stayed the same: keep complicated operations running, train good people, build SOPs that survive turnover, stay compliant, and don't let anything fall through the cracks.
